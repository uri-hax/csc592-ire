#### What sections are you writing?
Each section of a research paper follows different expectations and community adopted guidelines. After all of the papers we read this semester you should have a good feel for this. Is there an official set of steps? No, but many faculty and researchers have their own formulas and approaches for writing papers. So in essence, you will learn to write:
* Abstract (150 words)
* Introduction & Related Work
* Methods & System
    * Methods: describe the study to design and/or evaluate your system.
    * System: describe the system you are building and how it works.
* Results (Real or Hypothetical)
    * Real: If you have some results, this would include surveys, qualitative feedback, or benchmarks. Impress someone with your techincal prowess achievements! :)
    * Hypothetical: If you do not have results, then you can discuss what you would expect to find. Yes, you will make up some results for evaluating your system.
* Conclusion 
    * Future Work
    * Broader Impacts
* Bibliography (5 citations minimum, it is auto-generated)

Sounds like a lot of sections? It is, but we are keeping the word count and pages short.

### What will you be doing?

You (or, your group) will present your system to the class. You will have 6 minutes to present/demo and 3 minutes for questions. You will be graded on your presentation and demo.

### Goals: Your Presentation, Slides, and Demo

:::tip[Goals: Presentation (6 minutes long!)]
1. You will create slides to present your system and progress.
2. You will also demo your system.
    - Is it available online? (share a link you can select from the slides)
    - Did you record a video or screen capture? (upload a private link to YouTube and email the link with you slides)
:::

### Recommended Presentation Structure

The primary focus of the presentation is showing what you built. What was your contribution and how much progress you made. Discuss the technical challenges you faced and how you overcame them. 

##### Introduction and Problem Statement or Research Question (1-slide - 30-45 seconds)
- Start with something fun to grab everyone's attention.
- Briefly describe the problem or research question you are addressing. You do not have to answer the research question this semester.
- Possibly, mention why existing solutions fall short.

##### System Overview (1-slide - 1-2 minutes)
- Provide a high-level view of how your system works without diving too deep into technical details.
- Explain the main components and how they interact. (a diagram could be cool!)
- What is one really cool thing or technical challenge you overcame for your system?
- What was a key limitation or challenge you faced?

##### Demo (1-3 slides / or video - 2-3 minutes)
- Show off your super cool system!
- Use a video or include screenshots of your system in a slide. Or, you can do a live demo.

##### Conclusion (1-slide - 30-45 seconds)
- Discuss how you did or did not meet your goal from the propsoal. Did you meet your stretch goal?
- What are the next steps for your system?
- Possibly, what would you do differently next time?

### What will you be doing?

Writing a reflection and progress report in markdown (`checkin.md `) on how your system is going. You can reuse some of the writing from your abstract, proposal, and other parts of the class. ;)

:::note[Word Length!]
* This should be around 500 to 1000 words.
:::

### GenAI Usage

You may...
* Use genAI to help brainstorm ideas.
* Provide feedback on your ideas.
* You can use genAI to help plan your study design and help with analysis.
* Use it to make up details/data for your user data.
* Get ideas for other papers to read.
* Find more concise ways to phrase sentences. (basic grammar editing)

You may not...
* Use genAI to generate text for you paper.
* Use genAI to shorten text you have written.
* Should not use it to summarize what a research paper says.


#### What sections are you writing?

A couple of small sections (headers in markdown should be done with 1--5 `#` -> `### hi mom this is a header`)

```
### What has went right?


### What has went wrong?


### Changes to your plan from the proposal?


### What can you change to improve your progress?


### What can we (your peers and shaun) do to help?


```

You can cover if you need to change your tech stack, research question, etc.
It is totally normal to pivot your system and research direction during a project. This is a check-in to provide accountability and help us help you! :)

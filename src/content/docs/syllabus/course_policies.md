### What technology will you use?
The course will use GitHub, Google Forms, Markdown, and other fun technologies we choose to use as a class or you do as an individual in your milestones. This is a collaborative research course, so milestones are intentionally less structured so you have the freedom to be creative and explore your ideas.

## Class Etiquette
Please be respectful of yourself and others. Class activities and discussions should be taken seriously. They are essential to practicing the skills and thought processes required to excel. Please give space and respect to others to share and voice their opinions. Please give everyone in the class space to speak and voice their views.

### Attendance
Students are expected to attend class and participate in class activities. Occasionally, students may miss class activities due to illness, severe weather, or sanctioned University events. If ill, students should not attend class and should seek medical attention, especially if they have a communicable disease such as influenza (flu). Students should not attend class when the University announces classes are canceled due to severe weather. Also, it is the policy of the University of Rhode Island to accord students, on an individual basis, the opportunity to observe their traditional religious holidays. Students desiring to observe a holiday of special importance must inform each instructor and discuss options for missed classes or examinations. See Sections 8.51.11 – 8.51.14 of the University Manual for policy regarding the make-up of missed classes or examinations.

:::caution[What if there is bad weather?]
If the university is open but travelling to campus in a timely manner is not feasible (in the case of bad weather), Professor Wallace might conduct individual class or lab sessions hybrid or fully remote. If this happens, an announcement will be made by 1:00 PM the day of class. If there is no announcement, then assume everything will be help in person.
:::

:::note[What if you are sick?]
Please contact Professor Wallace and we can make accomodations. It is okay to stay home and get better! In most cases we can run the class in a hybrid setting to ensure you can participate normally. 
:::

### Diversity
We will celebrate and encourage our different perspectives in this course! :)

We live in a constantly evolving world. Our perceptions and experiences are growing in positive ways. We all come from unique backgrounds and places. Thus, our misconceptions and prior knowledge will be different. And that is okay. Only together can we forge forward.

Our differences (race, gender, class, sexuality, religion, ability, etc.) are our greatest strengths. We will create a learning environment that supports a diversity of thoughts, perspectives, and experiences:
* If you have a name and/or set of pronouns, please let me know! :)
* I (like many others) always learn about diverse perspectives and identities. If something was said in class (by anyone) that made you feel uncomfortable, please talk to me about it. (Again, anonymous feedback is always an option).
* I want to be a resource for you. If something is impacting your class performance, please talk with me. You can always submit anonymous feedback (anonymous feedback form). With anonymous feedback, I can make a general announcement to the class to address your concerns.

### Academic Honesty Policy
All submitted work must be your own. If you consult other sources (class readings, articles or books from the library, articles available through internet databases, or websites), these MUST be properly documented, or you will be charged with plagiarism and will receive an F for the individual assignment. In some cases, this may result in a failure of the course as well. In addition, the charge of academic dishonesty will go on your record in the Office of Student Life. If you have any doubt about what constitutes plagiarism, visit the following websites: the URI Student Handbook and Sections 8.27.10 – 8.27.21 of the University Manual (<a href="web.uri.edu/manual/" target="_blank">web.uri.edu/manual/</a>).

### Cheating and Plagiarism
Any task, assignment, or milestone that has been specified as Individual Work must be your own work. While you may discuss general solutions and design ideas with classmates. **YOU MAY NOT**: 
* share code with other students
* look at any other student’s code
* use code provided to you by anyone else
* use code that you find on the Internet
 
If you use code that has been provided for you by the instructor or that you created prior to the start of this course, include in your comments where the code came from. If you ever have a question about what is acceptable when working on a programming assignment, please contact your instructor.

**Generative AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.** are very good at writing introductory-level computing code. While these tools can be useful to programmers solving large problems we want you to learn the programming fundamentals. Therefore, you are expected to write all code on Individual Work without genAI generating code for you. Do not trust the output of these tools at face value. Just like you would not trust every post on StackOverflow. You could imagine these tools as having a lab partner who sometimes provides good advice, but also bad advice.

You may use generative AI tools in a tutorial fashion where you ask general questions about coding and problem-solving. But you MAY NOT use them to do your milestones. When doing work for this course, follow these rules if you are using generative AI tools:
* You may consult the AI tools on general questions
* You MAY NOT copy and paste your code into the tool
* You MAY NOT copy and paste any results from the tool into your milestones

If you use an AI tool while working on an assignment, you **MUST** provide the prompt that you used and the result of the prompt as documentation in your assignment. Submit a file genai.md with your assignment.

:::note[Will we be using Gen AI in our Milestones?]
Yes we will! We will be using AI to augment our abilities as Computer Scientists and researchers. Each milestone will contain specific instructions about how and when to use gen AI.
:::

:::danger
Any violation of these rules may result in a grade of 0 on the task, assignment, or milestone. Also, you may be reported to the Dean and the Office of Student Life. See the University Manual for more information about the potential consequences of cheating. https://web.uri.edu/manual/chapter-8/chapter-8-2/."
:::
